Hello world — why I started this site.

Welcome to my corner of the internet. I built this site to have one place for the things I'm working on, the things I'm learning, and the occasional thought I want to keep around longer than a story.

What you'll find here

Mostly short write-ups about my projects: games, embedded and hardware work from university and the BEARS rocketry team, and things I figure out along the way. I'd rather post something useful and short than wait for a perfect long essay that never ships.

How it's built

This site is plain HTML and CSS: no framework, no build step, served with a custom domain.
